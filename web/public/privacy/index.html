Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Otter does not collect, store, or share your personal data. Files you send or receive move directly between your own devices on your local network. They never pass through our servers.

What Otter does on your device

  • Finds other devices running Otter on the same Wi-Fi network, and lets you choose one to send files to.
  • Sends and receives files over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) between your devices.
  • Keeps a local history of transfers on the device, which you can clear at any time from the app.
  • Optionally runs a web page on your Wi-Fi so a browser on another device can upload or download files. It is only reachable by devices on the same network.

Permissions and why we ask

  • Local network and Wi-Fi state: to discover nearby devices and to show your device's network address.
  • Storage and media: to read the files you choose to send, and to save received photos and videos to your Gallery or Downloads when you tap Save.
  • Notifications: to show live transfer progress and the status of the background service.
  • Foreground service: keeps Otter reachable while it runs in the background, so nearby devices can send you files.

You can revoke any permission in system settings. Otter continues to work for everything that does not need it.

Data we collect

None. Otter has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting that leaves your device.

Third parties

Otter does not share data with third parties because it does not send data anywhere. The website that hosts this page is served by Google Firebase Hosting and may log standard request information, as described in Google's privacy policy.

Children

Otter does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue at github.com/elmeeee/Grasp-drop/issues.
